## Dev Notes
- **File Location**: `autoBMAD/docuswarm/node_execution/context_builder.py`
- **Related Changes**: This story depends on Story 28.1 (contracts.py simplification) being completed first
- **Field Migration**: Task-related data will now be read directly from `NodeLoader().load(node_id)` by consumers (isolation.py, contract_builder.py, dual_agent.py) as specified in Stories 28.3 and 28.4
- **Runtime Fields to Retain**: The 9 fields that must remain are:
  1. `pipeline_id`
  2. `node_id`
  3. `node_name`
  4. `node_order`
  5. `original_context`
  6. `chained_deliverables`
  7. `shared_context`
  8. `iteration_feedback`
  9. `docs_context`
- **No Backward Compatibility**: Do not maintain deprecated field access or dual-read mechanisms - this is a clean removal
- **Ralph's Principles**: Follow DRY (eliminate duplication), KISS (simple return structure), YAGNI (no future-proofing for removed fields)
