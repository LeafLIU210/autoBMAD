## Dev Notes
- The PO node is the final node in the BMAD pipeline, responsible for breaking down architecture into actionable epics and user stories
- The `skill_ref` field connects this node to the BMAD skill system, enabling the IndependentAgent to invoke the appropriate skill
- The persona "David" should embody a strategic product mindset with expertise in prioritization frameworks
- Ensure backward compatibility: nodes without `skill_ref` should continue to work (skill_ref is optional per NodeTaskConfig)
- The `role_supplement` text will be injected into the system prompt to guide the agent's behavior
- Refer to Story 32.1 for the NodeTaskConfig dataclass changes that enable skill_ref support
- Coordinate with Story 32.7 to ensure the skill_ref is properly integrated into IndependentAgent prompt construction
